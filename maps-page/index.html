§ How it works

Two modes, one explorer.

The explorer has two browsing modes — switch between them via the tabs at the top of the sidebar.

01

Individual model mode

Browse the BHE simulation output for any single CMIP6 climate model. Each map contains all 6 togglable layers covering the 50-year and 100-year horizons.

  • Models: BBC, CanESM, GFDL, GISS, HadGEM, IPSL, MIROC, MPI
  • Scenarios: SSP 2-4.5 and SSP 5-8.5
  • 16 maps total (8 × 2)
02

Ensemble mode

Combine all 8 models into one map showing the per-pixel statistic — Mean, 25th percentile, Median (P50), or 75th percentile. Reveals the spread of model predictions across the ensemble.

  • Statistics: Mean (μ), P25, P50 (Median), P75
  • Scenarios: SSP 2-4.5 and SSP 5-8.5
  • 8 maps total (4 × 2)

About these maps

All maps are folium HTML files generated directly by the BHE simulation notebook. The website's explorer loads them on demand based on dropdown selection — so what you see is the authentic, full-resolution thesis output.
