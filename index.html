§ About

My work bridges geosciences, geoinformatics, and renewable energy. As an M.Sc. graduate from KIT, I developed Python-based analytical models that combine CMIP6 climate projections with borehole heat exchanger physics to quantify how a warming climate reshapes geothermal energy potential across Germany.

Alongside research, I work as a web developer at IONOS SE, building responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript templates and integrating AI tools (Claude, Copilot) with MCP servers. I previously contributed to geothermal exploration projects at Hydrosion GmbH (Bruchsal Geothermal Power Plant — EnBW) on lithium-from-brine investigations, and supported lab analyses on borehole heat exchangers at KIT.

I'm passionate about turning complex spatial data into clear, accessible visualizations, the energy transition, and finding the intersection between sustainability, technology, and impact.
