How to read the maps.

01

Click any card to open the live map

Each map opens in a new tab. Use mouse-wheel or pinch to zoom; click and drag to pan around Germany.

02

Toggle layers in the top-right

Each scenario has multiple layers (mean, 25th/50th/75th percentiles). Switch between them via the layer control.

03

Hover or click for values

Hovering over a region shows the heat-extraction rate or power output for that grid cell in W/m or W.

04

Compare scenarios

Open two tabs side-by-side (one SSP 2-4.5, one SSP 5-8.5) to compare regional differences directly.

About these maps

All maps were generated from the BHE simulation notebook using folium / Leaflet.js. Underlying data: 8 CMIP6 GCMs at ~5 km resolution, processed via Google Earth Engine and bounded by Germany's EPSG:4326 shapefile.
